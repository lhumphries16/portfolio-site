# Approved strategic decisions

These are decisions already made with Tre. Do not reopen them unless the existing implementation reveals a genuine technical conflict.

## Brand
- Public-facing brand: **Tre Humphries**.
- Do not create a separate agency/studio name.
- Do not imply a team, office, departments, or agency scale.
- Tre is comfortable appearing personally on the site, including a portrait where it improves trust.
- The site should feel like a premium independent professional practice, not a personal-brand influencer page.

## Two buyer surfaces, one reputation
- Web & Digital is the actively marketed acquisition lane.
- Controls Engineering is a separate, serious technical sales surface.
- Do not create two brands.
- Do not make the homepage a crude 50/50 chooser that feels like two unrelated careers.
- Make each buyer path obvious and allow direct links to the relevant page.

## Web buyer
Primary buyer:
- established service businesses and local brands whose digital presence lags the quality/maturity of the real business.

The brand should be broad enough to fit:
- HVAC / restoration / plumbing / electrical / roofing / landscaping / contracting
- food/local brands
- beauty/personal services
- other credible owner-operated service businesses

Do **not** position Tre as “web design for contractors only” or “cheap small business websites.”

Contractors/technical service businesses may remain a priority outbound segment because Tre has unusually relevant technical credibility, but the public web brand should not exclude other strong local/service businesses.

## Geography
- Do not position the web practice as New Jersey-only.
- Tre may market in New Jersey, St. Louis/Missouri, Virginia, and other U.S. markets.
- Geography can be a quiet credibility detail, not the brand proposition.
- Do not create speculative local SEO city pages now.

## Web offer
Core front-door offer:
**Fixed-Scope Website & Search Foundation**

Commercial principles:
- fixed scope
- fixed fee
- client ownership
- clean launch/handoff
- no required retainer
- custom workflow/tool/search work can be scoped separately

Pricing presentation:
- public website engagements may say **“typically start around $3,000”**
- do not publish an hourly rate
- do not create Bronze/Silver/Gold packages yet
- do not put the $3,000 price on physical business cards

## Conversion
Primary CTA for web:
**Schedule a Project Call**

Secondary:
- Send Project Details
- Email Tre

Primary CTA for controls:
**Schedule a Controls Consultation**

Tre prefers scheduled calls and does not want unknown cold phone calls to be the primary intake path.
Do not call the CTA a “Free Consultation.”

## Visual direction
- polished and premium is required
- premium must appeal to real operating-business owners, not only designers/tech graduates
- controlled, intentional, commercially credible, technically literate
- strong typography
- generous whitespace without becoming sparse/sterile
- strong real project imagery
- restrained motion
- restrained palette and accents
- excellent desktop/mobile presentation

Avoid:
- generic marketing-agency aesthetic
- loud growth-hacker gradients
- fake analytics graphics
- stock-contractor clichés
- walls of icons
- excessive animation
- art-school cleverness that obscures the offer
- sterile SaaS minimalism
