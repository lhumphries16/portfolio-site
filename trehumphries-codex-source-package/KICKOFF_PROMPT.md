## Commercial priority

The active acquisition lane is Web & Digital for established service businesses and local brands. The site needs to make a $3,000+ fixed-scope web engagement feel credible.

At the same time, `/controls` must independently feel serious to an OEM, controls team, or engineering manager. Do not force either buyer to parse the other discipline before finding relevant proof.

## Important visual rule

Do not interpret “premium” as a sterile startup/design-school aesthetic. The web side should appeal to established operating businesses — contractors, restoration firms, landscaping/service companies, food/local brands, beauty/personal services, etc. It should feel commercially competent, polished, trustworthy, and expensive without looking like a generic marketing agency.

The controls side can be more restrained and technical while remaining part of the same visual identity.
